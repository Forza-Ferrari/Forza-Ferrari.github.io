About

Hello! My name is Yimu Wang, and I am an undergraduate student in the Turing Class of 2024 at Peking University. I am currently involved in research under the supervision of Professor Boxin Shi. My research focuses on video generation–related world models, with a particular emphasis on multi-view video generation.
